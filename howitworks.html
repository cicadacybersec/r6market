THE PROCESS

How SiegeVault Works

Every transaction is handled with precision. Each account is carefully vetted and verified before being made available for purchase.

01
INITIATION

Register Your Interest

Browse the available accounts and submit a registration form for the one that interests you. We ask for your display name and an email to reach you. All email communication will be handled by a vetted representative from their respective team. No payment, no commitment at this stage.

Takes less than 60 seconds.
02
VERIFICATION

We Reach Out & Verify - Human, never automated

We reach out personally to confirm your intent and answer any questions. A short verification ensures all parties are legitimate and protected from the outset. Our representatives will always contact you from their respective @siegevault.org email to help prevent impersonation and scams. This process is built to eliminate uncertainty, and establish a basis of trust before anything begins.

Usually within 24 hours.
03
AGREEMENT

Terms Are Agreed - Clear & Transparent

All details: price, payment method, and transfer timeline are agreed and documented before any transaction occurs. This helps protect both parties involved from any ambiguity or last-minute changes. We also retain a copy of the agreement for our records, and to help resolve any potential disputes that may arise. Everything is visible, confirmed, and controlled.

Full transparency at every step.
04
HANDOVER

Secure Account Transfer

Once payment is confirmed, we walk you through the full account handover. From credentials to email transfer, we ensure a smooth transition. At this point, the account is yours.

Clean. Complete. No loose ends.

Ready to move forward?
